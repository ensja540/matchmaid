Cleaners in Darwin - your questions

How much does a house cleaner cost in Darwin?

Cleaners on Match Maid set and show their own hourly rates up front, so you can compare before you contact anyone. Rates advertised across Darwin run from about $40 to $75 an hour, with most around $55 depending on the cleaner and the type of clean.

Are Match Maid cleaners in Darwin vetted?

Cleaners can add ID-verified, police-check and insured badges to their profile, so you can see who has been checked before you let anyone into your home. You always choose who to contact.

Is Match Maid free for households?

Yes - browsing, comparing and contacting cleaners in Darwin is completely free for households. You arrange the clean and payment directly with your cleaner, and Match Maid never takes a commission.

Which Darwin suburbs are covered?

All of them. Cleaners set the suburbs they travel to, and the search only shows you people who cover yours - so a result in Darwin is someone who will actually come to your address.
