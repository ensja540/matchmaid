What Match Maid is

A directory of local house cleaners, with the prices on show.

Match Maid is a marketplace for independent house cleaners and the households who want one. Every cleaner sets their own hourly rate and the suburbs they travel to, and both are on their profile before you get in touch. There is no bidding, no quote request that goes out to ten strangers, and no booking fee - you find someone you like, message them, and arrange the clean directly.

How it works

For households

Search your suburb and you only see cleaners who actually come to it. Compare their rates, reviews and badges - ID verified, police checked, insured - then message the one who fits your home. You pay the cleaner directly for the work, and Match Maid never takes a cut. It is free for households, and it stays that way.

For cleaners

Make a profile, set your rate, the cleans you offer and the suburbs you cover, and households in those suburbs can find you. You keep everything you charge: no commission on jobs, ever. Listing is free while we grow. List your services.

Questions

Is Match Maid free for households?

Yes. Searching, comparing and messaging cleaners costs nothing, and there is no booking fee or markup. You pay the cleaner their advertised rate, directly.

How much does a house cleaner cost?

Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and shows it on their profile, so you can compare before you contact anyone. A deep or end-of-tenancy clean costs more than a regular one, because it takes longer.

Are the cleaners vetted?

Cleaners can add ID-verified, police-check and insured badges to their profile, so you can see who has been checked before anyone comes into your home. You always choose who to contact.

Does Match Maid employ the cleaners?

No. They are independent and run their own businesses - Match Maid is the directory that helps you find them. You agree the clean, the time and the payment with your cleaner.
