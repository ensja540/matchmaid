Good to know

Cleaners in Auckland - your questions

How much does a house cleaner cost in Auckland?

Cleaners on Match Maid set and show their own hourly rates up front, so you can compare before you contact anyone. Rates advertised across Auckland run from about $25 to $50 an hour for a regular clean, with most around $40. A deep clean costs more, because it takes longer and covers more.

Are Match Maid cleaners in Auckland vetted?

Cleaners can add ID-verified, criminal-check and insured badges to their profile, so you can see who has been checked before you let anyone into your home. You always choose who to contact.

Is Match Maid free for households?

Yes - browsing, comparing and contacting cleaners in Auckland is completely free for households. You arrange the clean and payment directly with your cleaner, and Match Maid never takes a commission on the job.

Which Auckland suburbs are covered?

All of them. Cleaners set the suburbs they travel to, and the search only shows you people who cover yours - so a result is someone who will actually come to your address. We have pages for Ponsonby, Mount Eden, Remuera and more.

How often should I book a cleaner?

Most households on Match Maid book weekly or fortnightly. A regular slot with the same cleaner works out cheaper per visit than repeated one-offs, because they learn your home and stop starting from scratch - and it is the arrangement cleaners will hold open for you.
