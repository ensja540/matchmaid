What moves the price

Five things, in the order they matter

1. How often you book

This is the big one. A fortnightly cleaner maintains a home; a one-off resets it. The same house takes noticeably fewer hours on a regular slot, so the per-visit cost drops even though the hourly rate has not.

2. Regular or deep

A deep clean adds the oven, the inside of the fridge, interior windows, inside cupboards and a wall wash. It is priced higher per hour and takes two to three times as long.

3. Whether products are supplied

A cleaner who brings their own products, vacuum and mop is carrying a real cost, and it shows in the rate. If you would rather they used yours, say so before you book - some cleaners price it differently.

4. Verification and insurance

ID-verified, criminal-checked and insured cleaners generally charge more than someone starting out. That gap is the whole point: you can see who has been checked before you let anyone into your home.

5. How far they travel

Cleaners set a travel radius, and one who already works your street has no dead time to price in. It is why a local cleaner is usually the cheaper one, and why our search only shows people who cover your suburb.

Good to know

Common questions about price

Is it cheaper to book a regular cleaner than a one-off?

Per visit, yes. One-off and first cleans usually take longer and are often quoted as a deep clean. If you are weighing a one-off against a fortnightly slot, compare what the visit costs, not what the hour costs.

Should I pay hourly or a flat rate?

Hourly is the norm here, and the fairer of the two for a home nobody has seen yet. A flat price suits a job with a fixed scope, like an end-of-tenancy clean. Cleaners on Match Maid advertise hourly rates and agree anything fixed with you directly.

Do I tip a house cleaner in New Zealand?

No - tipping is not expected here. If you want to recognise good work, the things that actually help an independent cleaner are a review on their profile and a standing fortnightly slot.

What does Match Maid charge households?

Nothing. Browsing, comparing and contacting cleaners is free, there is no booking fee, and we never take a commission on a job. Cleaners keep 100% of what they charge you.

Why is there a minimum rate?

Cleaners cannot advertise below $20 an hour on Match Maid. A race to the bottom is bad for the person doing the work and, in the end, for the person paying for it - so the floor exists on purpose.
